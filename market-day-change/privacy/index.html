What this app is

Market Day Change is a free app for phones and tablets by Duplessis Innovations (Pty) Ltd (CIPC 2025/597983/07).

Children use it to run a stall on a school market day. They can:

  • add item photos and prices
  • tap items to total a sale
  • enter the notes and coins handed over
  • see the change

The package and bundle ID is com.duplessisinnovations.marketdaychange.

Accounts and where information stays

There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app has no backend and does not use the cloud.

Item photos, names, and prices stay only on the device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Personal information

The app does not collect, store, or share personal data.

It does not use analytics or tracking. It does not use an advertising ID, and it does not show ads.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to photograph items.

Choosing an existing photo uses the system photo picker, without broad access to the photo library.

Sales

The app does not keep a purchase history. Each sale clears when the child taps Done.

Children

Market Day Change is designed for children. It complies with:

  • the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in South Africa
  • the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States
  • the Google Play Families policy

Changes to this policy

Before any change to how the app handles data, we will update this page and the Last updated date.

Contact
