Questions

Is an account needed?

No account is needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It works offline.

Where do photos and prices stay?

Item photos and prices stay on the phone.

How do I change the currency?

To change the currency, tap the gear on the Home screen. That screen is titled Market Day.

How do I delete an item?

On the Home screen, tap Edit on the item. Then tap Delete item. The app asks “Delete this item?” and says it will come off the stall. Tap Delete to remove it, or Keep to leave it.
